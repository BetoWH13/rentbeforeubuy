Disclaimer & Affiliate Disclosure

General decision-support only: what this site can help you test, what it cannot establish, and how affiliate booking links work.

What this site is

Drive Before Buy is an independent editorial website for car shoppers. The core idea is simple: before making a large vehicle purchase, spend enough real-life time with the exact model or the closest useful rental you can find to test the questions a short dealer drive may not answer.

The core artifact is the Weekend Test Drive Checklist. Category guides, the rental-matching guide, and the Weekend Rental Cost Estimator support that workflow.

The content is general information and decision support. It is not individualized buying, legal, financial, tax, insurance, or mechanical advice.

Important limits

  • A rental is not the exact car unless it actually is the exact car. A same-model or close substitute can answer some daily-life questions, but trim, equipment, ride, powertrain, condition, and other details may differ.
  • A rental cannot approve a specific used vehicle for purchase. Condition, history, title, warranty, maintenance, and mechanical questions belong to the specific vehicle and should be verified separately using appropriate records, inspections, and qualified professionals.
  • The site does not predict long-term reliability, depreciation, resale value, insurance cost, financing outcomes, or total ownership cost.
  • The rental-cost estimator is arithmetic, not financial modeling. It totals user-entered known rental-test costs. It does not model future regret or tell you whether a rental or purchase is financially advisable.
  • Rental availability and assignment are controlled by third parties. A category booking may not guarantee a specific make, model, trim, powertrain, or feature set unless the provider expressly says it does.

What this site is not

  • Not a car dealer, broker, lender, insurer, mechanic, rental company, or vehicle-history provider.
  • Not a model-review publication claiming hands-on testing of specific year-and-trim combinations.
  • Not a source of universal "best car" rankings or guaranteed purchase outcomes.
  • Not a substitute for professional advice appropriate to your situation.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages contain marked affiliate links to Discover Cars or other third-party booking services. If you click a marked affiliate link and complete a qualifying booking, Drive Before Buy may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

The site does not control rental prices, availability, vehicle assignment, protection or insurance terms, cancellation policies, payment processing, or customer support. Any booking or rental agreement is with the relevant third party, not with Drive Before Buy.

The Weekend Car Test remains usable regardless of where you rent.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not turn a rental platform into the identity of the site. The editorial method is intended to remain useful even when the best answer is to use a dealer's extended test, borrow a comparable vehicle, rent elsewhere, or skip a rental because the available substitute would not answer the shopper's real question.
